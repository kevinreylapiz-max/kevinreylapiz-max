**Problem:** Explore how cacao-pod image classification and location-based records could be brought together in an Android application.

### SYNAPSE — Human-Directed AI Engineering Methodology and Toolkit

SYNAPSE is a local Python methodology and toolkit for structuring AI-assisted software engineering work. Its value is in operational use: it supported work on CacaoGuard throughout development and maintenance, rather than remaining a theoretical workflow proposal.

**Operational use:** Used during development, debugging, testing, thesis preparation, thesis defense, and post-defense maintenance.

**Workflow components:**

- Context continuity across many AI-assisted work sessions
- Checkpointing, including 100+ recorded checkpoints, plus project handoffs and growth journals
- Smoke-test workflows and a CR-based workflow
- Human-directed orchestration across multiple AI models

**Engineering boundary:** SYNAPSE structures context, work, and verification; the human engineer directs tasks, reviews results, and decides what is accepted. It is not an autonomous AI system or a claim of a production-ready agent framework. Checkpoints and workflow records do not by themselves establish correctness; validation depends on the checks and review applied to each change.

[Source repository (private)](https://github.com/kevinreylapiz-max/Synapse) · [AI-assisted engineering workflow](AGENTIC_WORKFLOW.md)
